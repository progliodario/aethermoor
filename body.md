## A new look for the whole game: anime art

The owner asked for graphics that are far richer and close to the look of an anime. Everything that is drawn was drawn again, and the rasterizer of both clients learned what a painter needs. Decision and alternatives: `docs/adr/0013-painted-anime-art.md`. A picture of the result: `docs/design/img/scene.png`.

### Rules (spec 13, v2)

- A layer can be **blurred** (`blur`), kept **inside what is already painted** (`clip`), **multiplied** or **added** (`blend`), and filled with a **noise** texture (value noise from an integer hash, optionally periodic).
- **Macro tiles**: a terrain recipe of `n * 32` units covers `n x n` tiles. **Ground blending**: a `blend` rank in the tileset makes a ground fade over its neighbours of a lower rank.
- A rig part has a second recipe and draw order for the **view from behind** (`art_back`, `z_back`); a creature recipe can say it `faces` left and is mirrored to face the player.
- `tools/reference/raster.py`: a reference rasterizer in Python. It builds 14 new conformance cases (blur, clip, multiply, add, noise, periodic noise) and draws the previews.

### Art (all regenerated by tools)

- **People**: 59 units tall and 4.6 heads instead of mascots; large eyes, hair with a ring of light, cel shading; eight protagonists and seven kinds of villagers with their own hair, hats and clothes; front and back views; new rig (a `cape` part), new idle and walk clips.
- **Ground**: 30 painted macro tiles (4 x 4 tiles each), walls, floors, door and bridge with matter and shade.
- **Props**: 22, with bark, stone, cloth, soft ground shadows and glows. Trees are larger.
- **Creatures**: 63 drawn by family (jelly slimes, beasts from the side, guardians in armor, eight single ones) plus the 8 Echoes recoloured from them.

### Clients (desktop and Android, same behavior)

- Rasterizer: blur, clip, blend, noise; works on the box of each layer instead of the whole canvas.
- A tile is 64 pixels instead of 48 (times the density); macro tiles; soft ground edges; the player seen from behind when walking up; beasts and companions that turn to face the player.
