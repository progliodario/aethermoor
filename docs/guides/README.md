# Guides

How-to guides.

- [Continuing the work](continue-the-work.md): the handbook for whoever picks up the project, a person or an AI: what to read, where the
  project stands, the loop of a session, a change through every layer (with the affix choice as the worked example), the checks and the
  generators, shipping, what to do when CI fails, working without compilers, working with the owner.

- [Adding content](add-content.md): an item, a skill, a protagonist, a recipe and a prop to gather from, a companion, a title, a prologue, a sound, a creature, a quest and its dialogue, a place (stamp and landmark), a region, a language, and the tools and checks that go with them.
- A protagonist (with its skills, weapon, look, story and personal quests) is described in [Adding content](add-content.md), section "A protagonist", and specified in [spec 18](../spec/18-protagonists.md).
