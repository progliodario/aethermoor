# Design documents

Narrative and design source of truth, in Italian. The first draft of world, protagonists and systems is in [`../plan/03-game-design.md`](../plan/03-game-design.md).

The cooldowns, the mana and the skills of the protagonist are described in [spec 05](../spec/05-combat-v1.md). The rules of the graphics are in [`art-style.md`](art-style.md). Generated from the data of the base pack (do not edit by hand): [`catalog.md`](catalog.md) (`tools/make_catalog.py`: regions, creatures, items, quests and sites in tables) and [`balance.md`](balance.md) (`tools/balance_report.py`: a rough model of every fight, to find outliers).

The protagonists, as they are in the game today, are in [spec 18](../spec/18-protagonists.md) and in the catalog of the pack. Planned files (to be split out of the plan as the design is approved): `gdd.md`, `world.md`, `regions/`, `protagonists/`, `companions/`, `antagonists.md`, `guild.md`, `reincarnation.md`, `tone-guide.md`.
